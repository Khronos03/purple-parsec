## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a nursery bench (mesón): every product stands on a concrete bench at its true relative height and width, with a paper price tag hanging from the bench edge. Refuses the equal-card product grid and the banner hero.

OWN-WORLD: Olive wall #547236, concrete bench in sage (#c7cac1 front, #dcded6 top), white paper tags with a punched hole and hairline string, olive ink. Zilla Slab for names and display, Hanken Grotesk for text. Photo frames are arches whose width:height is the product's real proportion. Botanical sprig line art on the wall. No shadows except tag lift.

STORY: The visitor sees how big each planter really is next to the others, reads its tag (name, medidas, desde $), opens one, picks an acabado on the large tag, sees the price restamp, taps WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Olive wall; wordmark header; "RUSTIC HOME" slab + slogan + "Ver catálogo" and WhatsApp; a bench across the full width carrying 4 (mobile) to 6 (desktop) planters at one shared scale with a cm ruler on the left and height tags hanging below.

FORM: Vivero / mesón con etiquetas — candidate 5 of 7 (1 muestrario del taller, 2 catálogo impreso, 3 plano con cotas, 4 revista de interiores, 5 vivero, 6 lámina botánica, 7 tablero de plaza). seed:873eef904f0d29ac (degraded roll, no challengers). Signature interaction: true cm scale on the hero bench only (catalog photos are uniform 4:5 arches, user decision 2026-11-04); tags swing from their hole on hover/focus; on the product page the big tag is the order form and the price restamps on change.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
